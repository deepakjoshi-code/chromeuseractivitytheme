Not requested, and never will be: browsing history, bookmarks, cookies, downloads, or the contents of any page.

Always on, not optional

Aura refuses to react to anything that looks like health, money, legal trouble, job hunting, dating, adult content, politics or religion. When it sees one of those it scores nothing, stores nothing, and leaves the theme exactly as it was — because snapping to a “neutral” theme would itself tell anyone watching your screen that something private is on it. There is no switch for this, by design.

Aura is also disabled in Incognito windows and cannot be enabled there.

Erase everything

Clears settings, history, pins and rejections. Cannot be undone.
